# Privacy Policy for Calculadora Industrial

**Effective Date:** November 3, 2026

**Calculadora Industrial** ("the App") is developed and maintained by **Ambrosio** ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of our Android application (Package Name: `com.ambrosio.calculadora_industrial`).

---

## 1. Overview and Offline Data Processing

- **100% Offline Calculation Engines:** All core functionality and calculation features within Calculadora Industrial operate entirely on your device. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, calculation inputs, or user history to external servers.
- **No User Registration or Accounts:** The App does not require user registration, account creation, or login credentials. You can use all features of the App anonymously without providing any personal information.

---

## 2. Information Collection & Third-Party Services

While the core app features work 100% offline, the App integrates third-party services to support advertising display:

### Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
We use **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** to display banner advertisements within the App. Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-sensitive data, such as:
- Device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID).
- IP address (for general location or network diagnostics).
- App interaction, performance, and crash diagnostics data.

This data is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

---

## 3. Data Retention and Security

Because the App itself does not collect or store any personal user data on external servers, there is no personal data stored by us to retain or delete. Any calculation data remains strictly local on your device and is discarded when you exit or clear app storage.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

Calculadora Industrial is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data through a third-party service integrated into the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have choices regarding personalized advertising:
- You can reset or opt out of personalized ads via your Android device settings under **Settings > Google > Ads**.
- You can clear local application cache and data at any time through your Android device settings.
